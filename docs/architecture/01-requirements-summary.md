# Requirements Summary - Business Analysis

## Executive Summary

**Product Name**: AI Company OS (codename: 1Person)
**Vision**: Enable a single human founder to run an entire company powered by autonomous AI agents.
**Analogy**: "Shopify for ecommerce" → "AI Company OS for AI-run companies"

---

## Problem Statement

### Current Pain Points
1. **Solo founders are overwhelmed**: Too many tasks, limited time
2. **Hiring is expensive**: Traditional employees cost $50k-150k+/year
3. **AI tools are fragmented**: ChatGPT, marketing tools, analytics - all separate
4. **No unified AI workforce**: No platform manages AI agents as a team

### Target Solution
A unified platform where:
- AI agents work as a coordinated team
- Human founder acts as CEO, setting direction
- Agents autonomously execute, report, and improve

---

## Target Users

### Primary Persona: Solo Founder
- **Demographics**: 25-45 years old, tech-comfortable but not developer
- **Goals**: Launch business with minimal overhead
- **Pain Points**: No budget for employees, overwhelmed by tasks

### Secondary Personas
| Persona | Description | Key Need |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Creator | YouTuber, influencer | Automate brand deals, merch |
| Indie Hacker | Side-project builder | Launch MVPs faster |
| Small Business Owner | Local business | Digital marketing automation |
| Agency Owner | Marketing/dev agency | Scale without hiring |
