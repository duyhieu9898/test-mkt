
# AI Strategy Engine

Purpose:
Act as the decision-making brain of the AI company system.

The Strategy Engine analyzes:

- market intelligence
- company performance metrics
- experiment results
- competitor activity
- opportunities detected

Then it decides what the company should do next.

Core Loop:

Context Aggregation
↓
Strategic Reasoning
↓
Goal Decomposition
↓
Resource Allocation
↓
Execution Planning
↓
Agent Task Creation
