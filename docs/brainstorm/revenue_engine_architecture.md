# Revenue Engine Architecture

AI Company OS -- Revenue Generation Layer

## Purpose

The Revenue Engine helps AI-run companies generate real revenue by
automating acquisition, sales, and optimization workflows.

Goal: Increase revenue while reducing operational costs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Revenue Loops

Every business grows through three loops:

1.  Acquisition
2.  Conversion
3.  Retention

Revenue agents operate inside these loops.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Agent Types

## Growth Agent

Responsible for acquiring traffic.

Capabilities: - SEO content generation - social media distribution -
advertising campaigns - keyword research - competitor analysis

Outputs: traffic campaigns SEO pages growth experiments

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Lead Generation Agent

Responsible for identifying potential customers.

Capabilities: - prospect discovery - ICP detection - contact database
building

Outputs: prospect lists segmented audiences lead pipelines

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Sales Agent

Responsible for converting leads into customers.

Capabilities: - automated outreach - follow-up sequences - meeting
scheduling - deal tracking

Outputs: sales conversations qualified leads closed deals

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Conversion Optimization Agent

Responsible for improving conversion rates.

Capabilities: - landing page testing - funnel optimization - pricing
experiments

Outputs: higher conversion rates optimized funnels

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Revenue Pipeline

Business Goal ↓ Strategy Engine ↓ Growth Agents Acquire Traffic ↓ Lead
Agents Generate Prospects ↓ Sales Agents Convert Leads ↓ Conversion
Agents Improve Funnel ↓ Revenue Growth
