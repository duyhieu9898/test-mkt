
# Market Intelligence Engine

Purpose:
Continuously collect, analyze, and surface market signals to help AI-run companies adapt strategy.

Goal:
Give both the AI system and the CEO dashboard real-time awareness of:
- market trends
- competitor activity
- emerging opportunities
- potential threats

Core Pipeline:

Data Sources
↓
Market Scrapers
↓
Signal Processing
↓
Trend Detection
↓
Opportunity Engine
↓
CEO Insights Feed
↓
Strategy Engine
