
# Opportunity Detection Engine

Purpose:
Convert market signals into actionable business opportunities.

Evaluation Factors:

Demand Score
Search volume growth.

Competition Score
Number of existing tools.

Audience Fit
Relevance to company target market.

Opportunity Score Formula:

Opportunity Score =
0.4 demand +
0.3 competition gap +
0.3 audience fit

Example Result:

Opportunity: AI lead generation for plumbers
Score: 82
